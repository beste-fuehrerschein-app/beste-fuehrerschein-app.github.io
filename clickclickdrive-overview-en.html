What is ClickClickDrive?

According to the review, ClickClickDrive is aimed at learners who want to study flexibly and independently of a driving school. The review describes it as an official TÜV/DEKRA licence partner whose app contains the full, current 2027 question catalogue, including all video questions, traffic signs and number tasks.

Strengths

  • Full TÜV/DEKRA question catalogue - all classes (B, A, AM, truck, bus)
  • Smart learning logic: wrongly answered questions are repeated more often
  • Real exam simulation in the exact TÜV/DEKRA layout with a time limit
  • Readiness indicator shows when you are ready for the real exam
  • All 13 official exam languages (German, English, Arabic, Turkish and more)

Weaknesses

  • A premium subscription is needed for full exam simulations
  • Few advanced statistics in the free version

Features in detail

  • Offline mode: study without an internet connection
  • Detailed learning statistics and progress charts
  • Video questions with animated traffic scenes, as in the real exam
  • Free basic version with full access to all learning questions
  • Regular updates when the official question catalogue changes

Usability

The review says navigation is intuitive and you can start straight away without a long setup. The learning mode guides you through all topic areas, and the exam simulation looks identical to the real TÜV/DEKRA tablet. The review singles out the readiness indicator, which analyses learning progress and shows whether you are ready or still have gaps. The app runs on iPhones from iOS 14 and Android devices from Android 7.

Price and value

The free basic version gives full access to all learning questions in learning mode, with no time limit and no hidden restrictions. Premium (about €3-6 per month, depending on the offer) unlocks extended exam simulations, detailed statistics and an ad-free experience. For a typical study period of 4-6 weeks, the review puts the cost at €0 (free only) to about €12-18 with premium.

Who is it for?

According to the review, ClickClickDrive suits learners who study independently and flexibly, without being tied to a particular driving school: self-learners, multilingual learners and anyone who wants to avoid high upfront costs.

Choose it if you...

  • want to learn independently of a driving school
  • want to start for free
  • value multilingual support

The review says to skip it if you...

  • only study in German and other apps are enough
  • prefer the most-rated app
  • need over 68,000 ratings as a quality signal

ClickClickDrive FAQs

Is ClickClickDrive free?

Yes, the basic version is completely free, with full access to all learning questions. Premium (€3-6 / month) unlocks extended simulations and statistics.

Does ClickClickDrive use the official TÜV/DEKRA catalogue?

Yes. The review states that ClickClickDrive is an official TÜV/DEKRA licence partner and contains the current 2027 question catalogue including all video questions.

Does ClickClickDrive work offline?

Yes, after a one-time download the app works fully without an internet connection, for example on the subway or on the go.

Which languages is ClickClickDrive available in?

In all 13 official exam languages, including German, English, Arabic, Turkish, Russian and more.

Which licence classes is ClickClickDrive suitable for?

All classes: B, A, AM, truck (C/CE) and bus (D). The full catalogue of all classes is included.

How does ClickClickDrive differ from other apps?

With over 68,000 ratings it has the highest number of ratings of all universal apps, and it is one of the few to offer a real free full version.
